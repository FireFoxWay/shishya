SUNRISE GROUP OF SCHOOLS
Circular 2026/41 — Annual Examination Schedule

The annual examinations for Classes 6 to 9 will be held from 10 March to
24 March 2026. Papers begin at 9:30 AM and are of three hours.

Hall tickets will be issued from 1 March. A hall ticket is issued only to
students meeting the 75% attendance requirement, or holding a written
exemption from the Principal.

Class teachers must submit consolidated attendance registers to the office by
20 February. Any correction after that date requires the Principal's
counter-signature.
