# Rural Housing Assistance Scheme — Eligibility Rules (2026)

## Income ceiling
Annual household income must not exceed Rs 3,00,000. For applicants in
Scheduled Areas the ceiling is Rs 3,60,000.

## Land holding
An applicant owning more than 2.5 acres of irrigated land is ineligible.
Un-irrigated land is counted at half its area for this test.

## Prior benefit
A household that received assistance under the 2019 or 2022 rounds cannot
apply again before 2031.

## Required documents
Aadhaar, land record extract (Pahani), income certificate issued within the
last 6 months, and a bank passbook showing an active account.

## Processing
District office verifies within 30 working days. Applications missing the
income certificate are returned, not rejected — the applicant has 45 days to
supply it.
