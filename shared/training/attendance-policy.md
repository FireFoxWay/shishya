# Sunrise Group of Schools — Attendance & Promotion Policy (2026-27)

## Minimum attendance
A student must attend at least 75% of working days to be eligible to sit the
annual examination. Students between 65% and 75% may be permitted by the
Principal on medical grounds, with a certificate submitted within 15 days.
Below 65% there is no exemption.

## Promotion criteria
A student is promoted on passing in at least five subjects including the two
language papers. A student failing in exactly one subject may be promoted on
passing a re-examination held in June.

## Fee concessions
Siblings studying in the same school receive a 15% concession on tuition for
the younger child. Staff children receive 50%. Concessions are not additive —
the higher one applies.

## Transfer certificates
A TC is issued within 7 working days of a written request, provided all dues
are cleared. No TC is withheld for non-payment of transport fees alone.
